### Benchmark comparison (held-out test split, N=1005)

Table \ref{tab:benchmark} summarizes binary classification performance for three systems evaluated on the identical stratified test partition (matching training/validation splits). The BirdNET baseline applies a fixed confidence threshold of 0.5 on exported analyzer scores. The noise-aware MLP operates on BirdNET embeddings with identical thresholding (0.5) for binary decisions. The full pipeline applies autoencoder-based out-of-distribution rejection prior to the MLP; binary predictions follow the same 0.5 rule on classifier logits for non-rejected embeddings, while rejected embeddings are assigned the noise class.

Observed metrics — Baseline: accuracy 0.5154, F1 0.0000, FPR 0.0000, FNR 1.0000; MLP-only: accuracy 0.8090, F1 0.8033, FPR 0.1873, FNR 0.1951; MLP+AE: accuracy 0.8010, F1 0.7899, FPR 0.1718, FNR 0.2279.

### Statistical validation

We assessed paired differences in per-segment correctness (indicator equal to one if the predicted label matches the manifest-derived bird/noise label). A paired t-test and a Wilcoxon signed-rank test were applied to compare (i) BirdNET baseline versus the embedding MLP, and (ii) the MLP without versus with the autoencoder gate. For baseline versus MLP, the paired t-test yielded p = 1.9251e-44 (Wilcoxon p = 1.3480e-40); for MLP versus MLP+AE, the paired t-test yielded p = 1.0250e-01 (Wilcoxon p = 1.0247e-01). Values below the conventional α = 0.05 threshold are interpreted as evidence of a statistically significant shift in paired accuracy between systems on the same segments.
